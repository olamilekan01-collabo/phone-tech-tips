How to Make Your Phone Battery Last Longer

First, reduce screen brightness or turn on adaptive brightness. The screen can use a lot of battery power.

Second, check Settings for Battery usage to identify apps using significant power. Restrict background activity when appropriate.

Third, use Battery Saver when you need your phone to last longer. Settings vary by phone model and Android version.

What to Do When Your Phone Is Slow

Restart your phone, check available storage and update your apps from the official app store.

Remove apps you no longer use and clear an app's cache if needed. Avoid unknown cleaner apps that promise impossible results.

How to Protect Your Phone From Scams

Do not open suspicious links or share passwords, verification codes or banking PINs with anyone.

Install updates, use a screen lock and download apps from trusted sources. If a message claims to be from your bank, contact the bank through its official channels.
